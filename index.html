Tours in Your Phone’s Calendar

Subscribe once and your tours show up in Google or Apple Calendar — on your phone’s calendar app, your watch, in the car, and when you ask your assistant what you’re doing Saturday. It updates itself as you log swaps and overtime.

⚠️ Read this first. Everything else in this app is encrypted so that even I can’t read it. This feed can’t be — Google has to be able to read it to show it to you. Anyone who gets the link can see your tour schedule, so treat it like a password. It carries tours only — never your personal events, notes, overtime dollars, or anyone else’s information.
Your private calendar link
Google Calendar (do this on a computer — the phone app can’t add subscriptions):
  1. calendar.google.com → Other calendars + → From URL
  2. Paste the link → Add calendar
iPhone: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar → paste.

It is not instant. Google decides when to re-read the link — usually every several hours, sometimes up to a day. Square Root is always right the moment you change it; your phone’s calendar catches up later. Apple lets you pick the refresh rate.

Comp Time

Veterans bank comp time on Memorial Day and Veterans Day — the hours of your tour that actually fall on the holiday itself. Spend it by setting a tour to Veteran comp in that day’s sheet — that selector only appears once this box is ticked. Ordinary Comp time is a separate pot and never touches these hours.

Backup & Restore

Your calendar lives on this device. Back it up so a new phone, a cleared browser, or a lost device won't wipe it.

1 · Save a backup

Copying the code is the surest way — paste it into Notes, email it to yourself, or drop it in a password manager.

2 · Restore onto a device

Open this calendar on the other device, then paste your code or pick your file. This replaces whatever is on that device.

3 · Cloud backup OFF

Auto-saves an encrypted copy to the cloud after every change — only your recovery code can unlock it. On a new or wiped phone, enter the code to get everything back.
